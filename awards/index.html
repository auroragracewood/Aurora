How judging works

Transparent enough to be trusted. Selective enough to matter.

1. Eligibility Check

We confirm the URL works, the project is real, the applicant has authority to submit it, and required materials are included.

2. Rubric Review

Entries are scored against category-specific criteria: design, usability, marketing clarity, influence, proof, originality, and impact.

3. Outcome

Each entry is scored against the rubric. Finalists are named at the close of every monthly review window. Honorees and Winners are announced at the year-end ceremony.

4. Badge Delivery

Recognized entries receive an embeddable digital badge. Finalist badges ship the moment finalists are named. Honoree and Winner badges ship together at year-end.
